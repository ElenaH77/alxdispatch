What Dispatch Heard in November

Pedestrian, cyclist, and scooter strikes — vulnerable road users (VRUs) — plus crashes on corridors of interest (currently Braddock Road, Mount Vernon Avenue, Russell Road, and Commonwealth Avenue) on Alexandria’s public dispatch audio in November 2026. This log is very much in progress. Cards reflect what dispatch said in the moment; details and verdicts may be revised as more information surfaces. A man on a bike was struck by the driver of a dark blue Dodge sedan at North Columbus Street and King Street in Old Town at 1:48 on a Sunday morning, reported by a passing Uber driver. With both parties gone, this strike is unlikely to appear in any official record. Off the watched corridors; VRU strikes are tracked citywide.

A driver in a Honda CR-V hit a 13-year-old boy in the crosswalk at East Oxford Avenue and Mount Vernon Avenue (Del Ray) late on a Monday morning, injuring his left side and hip. He was conscious and walking when units arrived, and Medic 206 reported transporting one patient. Dispatch sent the call out as a hit-and-run, but a minute later reported all parties still on scene, and nothing on the radio shows anyone leaving; this card does not count it as a hit-and-run. ALXnow reports a silver 2003 CR-V; the radio confirms a 2003 Honda. Which way the driver was turning and the state of the signal did not air. The second child struck on this log in a week, after the GW Middle School student (October 28), and the same corner where a DASH bus strike was logged in July. The vehicle’s tag, read on a separate transmission, is withheld. Dot offset ~25 m south along Mount Vernon Ave to avoid stacking on the July dot.

Police dispatched it as a hit-and-run, but callers said the driver stayed, and officers ran the Prius’s tag at the scene, so this card does not count it as one. Officers closed East Braddock Road eastbound. The same block as the April 22 crosswalk hit-and-run that started this project. Dispatch said he may have a broken leg. The driver stayed on scene. Units were told to slow their response four minutes later; no transport or injury outcome aired. The third child struck on this log in ten days, after the GW Middle School student (October 28) and the 13-year-old at E Oxford Ave (November 2), all on Mount Vernon Avenue in Del Ray.

Wednesday, November 4 · 5:14 PM · Del Ray · Commonwealth Ave corridor

Commonwealth Ave & W Windsor Ave

Vehicle crash
Crash 8, I can take the crash at Commonwealth and West Windsor.
5:14:54 PM · APD · 4s · a traffic officer takes the crash at Commonwealth Ave & W Windsor Ave

A crash at Commonwealth Avenue and West Windsor Avenue in Del Ray at dusk on a Wednesday. Dispatch aired only one line, a traffic officer taking the call; no fire or EMS dispatch and no injuries on the air. According to an account sent to the author with a photo taken at the time, a car crossing Commonwealth westbound on Windsor appeared to have been t-boned by a driver traveling south on Commonwealth; the photo shows heavy damage along the westbound car’s left side. That damage is on the side away from a southbound car, which led the witness to believe a third vehicle may have struck it and left the scene; nothing on the radio confirms a third vehicle, so this card does not count it as a hit-and-run. (The automatic transcript heard “West Windsor” as “West Spencer”.)
