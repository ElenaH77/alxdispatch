An 11-year-old girl was struck and pinned underneath a blue Toyota RAV4 at 600 North Ripley Street at Holmes Run Parkway (West End), just after 7 PM. Several 911 calls came in at once; the response was upgraded to an entrapment and run as a formal incident command on 2 Delta — a tower ladder and rescue company lifted the pressure off her, airbags staged, and she was freed in about six minutes, conscious and alert, with a possibly broken leg, then transported to Fairfax, the regional trauma center. The driver remained on scene. The fourth child struck by a driver in Alexandria since September 3 — and the same West End pocket as September’s two North Pegram Street strikes. Off both watched corridors; VRU strikes are tracked citywide. Update (October 12): ALXnow reports she was pinned under the front axle, that the first-due crews — with heavy extrication equipment not yet on scene — improvised with ground ladders for leverage to lift the SUV, and that she was taken to Children’s National Hospital (the radio traffic at 7:25 PM had the medic heading for Fairfax) and is expected to make a full recovery. Per the APD crash report presented to BPAC (October 12, 2026): the child “was playing and ran into the roadway from in between two parked cars” when struck; the driver was not cited. (The report places it in the 700 block; dispatch and ALXnow say 600.)
