An 11-year-old girl was struck and pinned underneath a blue Toyota RAV4 at 600 North Ripley Street at Holmes Run Parkway (West End), just after 7 PM. Several 911 calls came in at once; the response was upgraded to an entrapment and run as a formal incident command on 2 Delta — a tower ladder and rescue company lifted the pressure off her, airbags staged, and she was freed in about six minutes, conscious and alert, with a possibly broken leg, then transported to Fairfax, the regional trauma center. The driver remained on scene. The fifth child struck by a driver in Alexandria since September 3 — and the same West End pocket as September’s two North Pegram Street strikes. Off both watched corridors; VRU strikes are tracked citywide.
