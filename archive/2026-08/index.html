A female cyclist struck by a black RAV4 at Duke Street and South Washington Street (Old Town). The driver stayed and waited for police; the woman got up and walked off northbound, disoriented, before medics could reach her — so fire logged it “property damage only” for lack of a located patient. She was struck all the same. Detail from the AFD 2 Bravo response channel. The second vulnerable-road-user strike at this Duke & South Washington corner in two months (a pedestrian was struck here on June 22).
